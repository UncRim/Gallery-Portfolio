Web redesign · 2025

From a strategy website to a conversion system

A HubSpot CMS rebuild of the T Shaped website: outcome-first story, early proof and one clear next step, so leaders book a Fit Call instead of bouncing.

Role
Lead UX/UI Designer · HubSpot CMS · Analytics (GA4/Hotjar)
Tools
  • HubSpot
  • Figma
  • ChatGPT
Type
Web redesign · 2025

Problem

Prospects couldn't answer: What do you do? Does it work? What's my next step? CTAs were buried and load times sluggish.

T Shaped runs leadership sprints for executives, but their website buried the point. Prospects couldn’t answer three basic questions: what do you do, does it work, and what should I do next?

I rebuilt the entire site in HubSpot CMS, restructuring the story around outcomes, surfacing proof early, and cutting the path from first visit to booked Fit Call.

Results

  • −19 pts bounce rate

    Home page bounce rate dropped after the rebuild.

  • +22% primary CTA clicks

    Call-to-action engagement increased post-launch.

  • +34% newsletter open rate

    Email performance lift in the first 30 days.

  • 0 post-launch 404s

    Clean migration with redirects and QA complete.

From static brochure to booking engine

Leaders don’t buy “strategy websites.” They buy clarity + proof. Behavioral data (Hotjar) showed visitors hovering on services and testimonials while skipping long prose. We reframed the page around the four questions prospects actually ask: What will I learn? Who’s teaching this? What can I do after? Where’s the proof? Then we rebuilt in HubSpot with modules that map to that decision path and a single, unmissable next step.

Approach

  1. Discovery & dataAudit GA4 events, Hotjar heatmaps/scroll maps, GSC; identify friction and dead zones.
  2. Story & IATranslate outcomes into a skimmable narrative; map a shortest-path funnel to “Fit Call.”
  3. Wireframes → Figma componentsBuild a reusable section library (hero, tiles, proof, FAQs, capture, footer).
  4. Content & microcopyOutcome-led headlines, plain-language FAQs, social-proof pull-quotes.
  5. Build in HubSpotDrag-and-drop modules, schema, redirects, perf budgets, and QA.
  6. Instrumentation & experimentsEvent tagging, scroll-depth, CTA variants, and module order tests.

Solution (what I changed)

  • Outcome-first hero with one primary action (“Book 15-min Fit Call”).
  • Service Library (6 tiles) to self-select the right sprint, with highly scannable entry points.
  • Video-first testimonials (leaders speaking to outcomes) for instant proof.
  • Mid-page micro-CTA for visitors convinced early; FAQ accordion to kill objections.
  • Newsletter capture (“Performance Review Playbook”) as a low-friction nurture.
  • Trust marquees (logos + benefit bullets) near decision points.
  • Modular HubSpot sections so marketing can reorder/A-B test without dev.
  • Performance passes (SVG, compressed media, CDN) to drop first contentful paint.

A better beginning

This wasn’t a paint job, it was a behavior redesign. By replacing generic claims with outcomes, proof, and one decisive action, we reduced cognitive load and gave buyers control. Modular sections let marketing iterate without waiting for dev, while analytics close the loop on what actually moves people. The real win: a site that respects attention, answers fast, and converts curiosity into real conversations.
