Master in AI (Katz School) Page Redesign

A decision-first redesign of the Katz School Master’s in AI program page on Drupal: outcomes, proof, then action.

Role
Web Designer (UX/UI) · IA · Content Strategy · A11y
Tools
  • Figma
  • Drupal
  • ChatGPT
Type
Web design · 2025

Problem

Prospective students couldn't quickly understand the program's value, outcomes, or next steps. The page felt dense and brochure-like.

Prospects don’t want adjectives; they want answers. I reframed the Katz Master’s in AI page to match their mental checklist: outcomes, proof, then action. Content is benefit-led, sections are modular, and every block either reduces uncertainty or advances a decision.

The goal: help prospects answer what will I learn, who’s teaching this, what can I do after, where’s the proof, and how do I take the next step?

Results

  • 1-scroll value + proof

    Outcomes, credibility, and actions visible above the fold.

  • 1-tap admissions path

    Simplified IA and sticky mobile CTAs reduce decision friction.

  • ↑ time on outcomes

    Visitors spend more time with program results and projects.

  • 0-dev content updates

    Modular sections marketing can reorder without breaking UX.

Reframing the decision

The original page read like a brochure: dense copy, buried proof, and no clear path from curiosity to application. I treated the redesign as a decision tool, not a content dump. Every section earns its place by answering a real prospect question or removing friction from the next step.

Approach

  1. Audit & goalsDefine the 30–60s decision the page must enable.
  2. IA redesignLinear flow: Hero → Why/Outcomes → Projects & Faculty → Admissions/FAQ.
  3. Content strategyRewrite headers in user language; trim copy to scannable chunks.
  4. ComponentsHero with proof chips, outcome cards, project tiles (video-ready), faculty grid, news strip, sticky CTAs.
  5. A11y & mobileOne type scale, AA contrast, left-aligned paragraphs, 44pt targets.
  6. HandoffAnalytics events (syllabus/advisor/FAQ/scroll) + test ideas for hero copy and CTA labels.

Solution (what I built)

  • Outcome-first hero with proof chips above the fold: value and credibility before the scroll.
  • Program highlights and student outcome cards that speak in results, not features.
  • Project tiles ready for video embeds; faculty grid for trust; news strip for freshness.
  • Sticky mobile CTAs and a simplified admissions path so the next step is always one tap away.
  • Modular Drupal sections marketing can reorder without breaking the decision flow.

Making the decision obvious

Higher-ed pages shouldn’t make visitors decode value. This redesign treats the page as a decision instrument: state the promise, prove it fast, and make the next step obvious, so qualified prospects move forward with confidence.
