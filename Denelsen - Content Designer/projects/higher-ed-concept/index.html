The Chapman Alumni Innovation Vault

A spatial-web concept that locks high-value university data inside an authenticated 3D vault, turning passive alumni readers into insiders.

Role
Product Designer (UX/UI & WebGL Prototype)
Tools
  • Blender
  • Gemini
  • Cursor
  • Figma
Type
Concept · 2025

Explore the Alumni Innovation Vault (opens in a new tab)

Problem

The Concept: If we lock high-value institutional data behind an “Authenticated Spatial Vault,” we can flip the psychology of the user from a passive reader to an exclusive insider.

The “Macrodata” Aesthetic: Leveraging a minimalist, “Severance”-style void environment to focus the user’s absolute attention on the 3D data and architectural models, eliminating the visual clutter of standard university websites.

Universities spend millions on campus expansion and cutting-edge research, but communicate these advancements to alumni through passive, 2D PDF newsletters and flat web portals.

Results

  • 0 installs · instant access

    Browser-based spatial environment with no download required.

  • 100% guided tour path

    Automatic camera path without manual panning.

  • 1-click virtual tour dive

    Cinematic camera dive into architectural models.

  • 1 in-flow mentor CTA

    Conversion action embedded within the immersive experience.

The iterative process & UX refinements

Iteration 1

The Conceptual Void & Social Proof

Initial state
Built an infinite, pitch-black WebGL grid with floating abstract math geometries. Explored “ghost avatars” (proximity chat) to simulate the networking aspect of alumni events.
The pivot
The sci-fi aesthetic was too alienating for an academic institution.
Iteration 2

Architectural Grounding & Chiaroscuro Lighting

The problem
The pure white 3D objects were blowing out the camera exposure, and the global lighting washed out the contrast.
The solution
Transitioned to an “Architectural Exhibition” vibe. Replaced abstract shapes with 3D building maquettes. Applied a warm, matte “Clay/Sand” material to retain detail without blinding the user. Tightened the WebGL spotlights to mimic theatrical staging, guiding the user’s eye organically.
Iteration 3

Solving Spatial Fatigue (The UI Bridge)

The problem
Forcing non-gamers to manually navigate a 3D space causes drop-off. Furthermore, opening a 400px sidebar covered the 3D object, ruining the visual balance.
The solution
Programmed a dynamic camera.setViewOffset() to perfectly re-center the 3D model whenever the 2D UI drawer opened. Introduced the [ NEXT PROJECT ➔ ] spatial arrow to create a guided, cinematic tour without requiring manual camera panning.
Iteration 4

The Macro-to-Micro “Dive” (Virtual Tour)

The problem
How to show the interior of a building without crashing the browser with heavy 3D interior assets?
The solution
Engineered a cinematic camera-dive. Clicking “View Virtual Tour” swoops the camera into the 3D clay model, triggers a seamless fade-to-black, and launches a full-screen architectural video overlay, keeping the user trapped in the immersion while pushing the primary [ BECOME A RESEARCH MENTOR ] conversion button.
