Questions people ask

Are you available for content design projects?

Yes. I take on content design projects: product documentation, UX writing, style guides, release notes and content audits. Send a few lines about the product, the audience and the deadline through the form, and I reply by email.

What is the difference between a content designer and a UX writer?

A UX writer focuses on the words inside an interface: buttons, errors, empty states. A content designer also shapes the structure around them: what to say, in what order, on which page, and how it is measured. I do both, so the words and the journey they sit in stay consistent.

What kinds of documentation do you write?

Product and developer documentation, including API references, task-first guides, onboarding flows and release notes. I started in IT infrastructure, so I write for the person who is mid-task and needs the next step, not a lecture.

Do you work with teams outside Austin?

Yes. I am based in Austin, Texas and work with teams in any time zone, with written updates so nobody has to wait for a meeting.

What do you need to get started?

The product or feature in question, who reads the content, where it appears, and any existing style guide or analytics. If none of that exists yet, a content audit is a good first step.
